Hyss

Certified crew for live events, matched, scheduled and paid.

A live-event staffing marketplace: companies hire certified freelance crew, with competency-matched shifts, call sheets, realtime chat, invoicing and payouts. One Next.js codebase serves web, iOS and Android through Capacitor.

01 · The brief

Staff certified crew without the group texts.

Live-event staffing runs on group texts, spreadsheets and chased invoices. Event companies struggle to find crew with the right certifications, and freelancers juggle several calendars and wait weeks to be paid. Hyss puts the whole loop in one system: post an event, match by competency, confirm shifts, share call sheets and chat, invoice, and pay out.

  • Events, positions and shifts with an offer and confirm flow, multi-day events and reusable templates
  • Matching by competency and certification, with skill levels and certificate expiry
  • Two-way Google and Apple calendar sync, and realtime crew chat for each event
  • Invoicing and payouts on Stripe Connect, with seat plus usage-metered billing

Key decisions

  1. 01One Next.js codebase ships web, iOS and Android through Capacitor, with a custom native Android Credential Manager plugin where the ecosystem had a gap
  2. 02Types flow from Prisma through Zod and tRPC into the React Query hooks, so an API change that breaks the frontend fails at compile time
  3. 03Authorisation lives in layered tRPC procedures (public, signed in, freelancer or company, then per-event owner or editor), so it is part of the API's type signature
  4. 04Calendar sync runs through a queue table with row-level locking, retries and backfill, and stored OAuth tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM
03 · My part

What I worked on.

  • Type-safe Turborepo: 93 Prisma models, about 340 tRPC procedures, an Android Credential Manager plugin.
  • Stripe Connect core: payout onboarding, hybrid seat and usage-metered billing, per-invoice checkout links with three fee-split modes, idempotent webhook reconciliation.
  • Two-way Google and Apple (CalDAV) calendar sync with encrypted OAuth tokens and a retryable row-locked job queue; realtime per-event crew chat on Supabase Realtime.
  • Company-enforceable TOTP MFA with hashed recovery codes, tokenized document-access approvals, and CI running ephemeral-Postgres integration tests and Playwright E2E on every PR.
  • Invoicing pipeline: quote to approval to invoice lifecycle, server and client PDF generation, public share links with view tracking and reminders.
